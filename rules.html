Rules & Regulations

Tournament Format

  • This is a Franchise-Based 9-a-Side Night Cricket League with Player Auction.
  • There will be a total of 16 Teams participating in the tournament.
  • Each team will have a purse of 30K Free Coins for the player auction.

Match Rules

  • There will be 8 Overs in an innings for every match.
  • A player can bowl a maximum of 2 overs in any innings.
  • Only genuine bowling actions are allowed. Any suspect action will be reported.

Important Dates & Fees

  • Player Registration Link will be open on 15th October 2025.
  • The Joining Fee for each Franchise Team is 20K.

Code of Conduct

  • All players and team officials must uphold the spirit of cricket at all times.
  • The Umpire's decision is final and must be respected.
  • Any form of misconduct on or off the field will lead to strict disciplinary action.
